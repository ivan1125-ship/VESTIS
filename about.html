Our Story

Born from a love of craft

VESTIS began in a small studio in Copenhagen in 2018. Founder Elena Marsh had grown frustrated with fashion's disposability — the constant churn of trends, the poor construction, the lack of honesty about where things came from and who made them.

She set out to build a brand that operated differently. One that started with exceptional fabrics, worked with skilled artisans, and charged a fair price for something genuinely worth owning.

Seven years later, we ship to 42 countries. Our core principles haven't changed: source honestly, make well, and design for permanence over trend.

How We Make Things

Every seam has a story

We work with a tight network of family-owned mills in Portugal, Japan, and Belgium. Each fabric is tested for durability, texture, and how it ages — because we want our clothes to become more themselves over time, not less.

Our production runs are deliberately small. We make enough to meet demand, never to fill a warehouse. This lets us maintain quality at every step and avoid the landfill problem that plagues fast fashion.

Each garment is inspected by hand before it leaves the workshop. If it doesn't meet our standards, it goes back.

What we stand for

Our Values

Three principles that guide every decision we make, from fabric sourcing to packaging.

Sustainability

All our fabrics are certified organic, recycled, or responsibly sourced. We offset 100% of our shipping emissions and use compostable packaging throughout.

Craftsmanship

We partner with master artisans who have spent decades perfecting their skills. Every stitch, cut, and finish reflects a standard that machine production simply cannot replicate.

Transparency

We publish the names and locations of every factory we work with. You can see the full cost breakdown of every item. We believe you deserve to know where your money goes.

FAQs

What is your return policy?

We offer hassle-free 30-day returns on all unworn, undamaged items with original tags attached.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express 1–2 days. Free standard shipping on orders over $150.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to 42 countries worldwide. International shipping times vary from 5–12 business days.

Are your materials sustainable?

All fabrics are certified organic, recycled, or responsibly sourced. Our packaging is 100% compostable.
